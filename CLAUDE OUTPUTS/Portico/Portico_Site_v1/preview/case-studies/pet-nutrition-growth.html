Growth doesn’t wait for the operation to catch up. The work is building the foundation before the weight arrives.

The challenge

The company’s private equity backers saw a real opportunity: expand retail, kitchen, delivery, and e-commerce operations, all at once. But the supply chain and operations team was early in their careers, the business lacked efficient processes and systems, and the supply network had little room to scale.

Our approach

We started on the floor, in the warehouse and kitchens. None of this was a people problem; the team simply hadn’t been given the systems to grow into. We helped build the processes, partners, and people to carry the growth.

Growth would have broken the operation, so we fixed the basics first

  • Inventory counts weren’t always done

    Full count: so the numbers could be trusted

  • Transfers between locations weren’t closed out

    Close-out process: a simple step to close out transfers

  • Supplies had no par levels or usage reports

    Par levels: and usage reports to drive reordering

  • Too many vendors, and some weren’t performing

    Vendor consolidation: one strategic packaging and shipping partner

  • E-commerce had no fulfillment partner built to scale

    New 3PL: developed and managed to handle fast e-commerce growth
