The challenge

The firm has grown quickly, much of it by bringing new companies on board. Each one arrives with its own spreadsheets, trackers, and ways of running a project, so each project was set up differently, by whoever ran it, and no two could be compared side by side. Risks, decisions, and lessons learned lived in inboxes and meeting notes, and ideas, change requests, and business cases came in through several doors.

Our approach

We’re building the foundation that changes that: a standard project template, launched by Control Center, with every project rolling up to one portfolio view. The firm’s existing idea pool, business case intake, and portfolio review connect into the new flow instead of being rebuilt. Teams keep their judgment. Leadership gets one honest view.
