The challenge

A home health division was inviting new hires to orientation by hand, from a separate tracker. Recruiting data lived in another system, so onboarding was always a step behind. People slipped through the gap between recruiting and day one: one hire arrived without clearance, another started missing a required document, and anyone who missed orientation went onto yet another manual list.

Our approach

I built an onboarding engine in Smartsheet that finds every eligible hire automatically, keeps a person in the loop, and sends the right invitation on time. Automation does the sorting, but a person signs off on each hire before an email goes out.
