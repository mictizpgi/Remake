The challenge

The company was running its shop on legacy software, printed stickers, and a pile of spreadsheets. Every repaired unit passes through many hands: picked up from a customer, received, repaired, tested, packed, shipped, and billed.

Our approach

We built a Smartsheet Work App that follows every unit from the driver’s pickup to the final invoice. The people who do the work were at the table the whole way: live tests with the office, working sessions with their lead, and decisions made by the people who run the shop every day.

Every unit had five places to look before the Work App

  • Office bounced between RA tracker, ticket tracker, repair log and printouts

    Office Portal: one landing page with forms, reports and help

  • Status set by hand, easily out of step with reality

    Checkboxes, not dropdowns: checking “Release to Service” sets Status: In Service

  • No clear view of where a unit was, or for how long

    Role-based reports: technicians see their tickets; the office sees what’s ready to commit

  • Billing rebuilt the repair story from several sources

    PDF service order: each ticket becomes one, ready for billing
