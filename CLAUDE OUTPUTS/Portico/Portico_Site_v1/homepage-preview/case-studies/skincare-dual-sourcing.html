Sometimes the fastest way to fix a plant is to stop asking it to do everything at once.

The challenge

A skincare manufacturer couldn’t keep its promises to customers while its own plant was being repaired. The home plant had to keep up with customer demand and fix its own operations at the same time. It couldn’t do both well.

Our approach

The answer was to qualify products at third-party contract manufacturers. I led the supply chain team through qualifying each product at outside manufacturers. That would free up capacity so the plant could focus on getting healthy.
