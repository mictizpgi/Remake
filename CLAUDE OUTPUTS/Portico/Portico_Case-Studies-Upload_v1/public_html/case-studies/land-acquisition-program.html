The challenge

Every contact with a landowner is a legal record. On a program heading toward condemnation cases, those notes and their dates can become evidence. The setup, built fast under pressure, got the program moving, but couldn’t carry that weight.

Our approach

We re-architected it: one register of record, one project plan template, and role-based views where people actually work. Nothing went big-bang: the old environment keeps running, untouched, until the team is ready to retire it, and the two are reconciled before anything is switched off. On the pilot tracts, the only differences were a couple of known quirks in the source data.

The same work was tracked in several places, and nobody could say which was right
