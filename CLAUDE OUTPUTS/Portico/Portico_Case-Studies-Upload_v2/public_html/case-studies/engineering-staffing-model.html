The challenge

Engineering budgets had long been set with rules of thumb like square footage. Leaders needed a model they could defend, and that the sites themselves would believe.

Our approach

Not a ratio per square foot, but the actual work. I built a model from the work up, had every site check it line by line, and delivered it to leaders through Smartsheet. Excel workbook templates did the heavy lifting with each site’s building, asset, preventive maintenance, and service request data; Smartsheet carried the results where people work.

Two buildings of the same size can need very different staffing

The model accounts for what a ratio can’t see:

  • Building age and equipment
  • Travel between buildings
  • Construction support
  • Round-the-clock coverage
